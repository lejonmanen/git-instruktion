## Metacognitive awareness

*Your ability to recognize common traps in your thinking and problem-solve your way out of them.*

+ Forming
+ Dislodging
+ Assumption
+ Location
+ Achievement
+ Progression
+ Interruption
+ Mislead

---

### Old problems

**Forming**: you understand the problem, but you're using the wrong approach to solve it.

**Dislodging**: you realize your approach isn't working, but still struggle to change it.

**Assumption**: wrong question, right answer - you solve a different problem than you were asked to.

**Location**: you think you're almost done, but realize you skipped crucial problem-solving steps in the beginning.

**Achievement**: you keep trying to fix your code, when it needs a total overhaul.

### AI-introduced problems

**Progression**: you think you're keeping up, but because AI generates code beyond your understanding, you're actually falling behind.

**Interruption**: every time you try to focus, an AI code-completion tool throws a suggestion at you, breaking your train of thought.

**Mislead**: you trust a suggestion that seems right but actually takes you in the wrong direction.

---

*The goal is not to stop these problems, but to become aware of them when they happen.*

*Enjoy the process of learning.*
